## Reduced-cost transcription (continuous check)

`check_rc()` (`:203+`) compares the paper's reduced-cost formula (eq:tep-rc) against Gurobi's own
reduced cost on existing columns at the current duals, every iteration; the worst absolute difference
must be ≈ 0. This is a **transcription** check (coding risk), not a proof of the pricer's optimisation
(that is the small-instance enumeration check, step 2 of the framework).
